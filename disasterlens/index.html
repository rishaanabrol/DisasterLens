Emergency Preparedness

Disaster Survival Guide

Knowledge is your best defense. Watch the situational protocols below and learn critical survival tactics for immediate response to major environmental and structural hazards.

FLOOD / WATER SURVIVAL

Seek higher ground immediately. Do not walk, swim, or drive through swift-moving floodwaters—just six inches of moving water can knock you down, and one foot can sweep your vehicle away. Stay off bridges over fast-moving water and always follow local evacuation orders immediately.

FIRE / BLAZE SURVIVAL

Crawl low under any smoke to your exit—heavy smoke and poisonous gases collect first along the ceiling. Before opening a door, feel the doorknob. If it is hot, leave the door closed and use your second way out. Once outside, stay outside and call 911 immediately.

STRUCTURAL COLLAPSE

Drop, cover, and hold on under a sturdy desk or table to protect your head and neck. If trapped under debris, cover your mouth with clothing to prevent dust inhalation. Tap rhythmically on a pipe or wall so rescuers can hear you. Only shout as a last resort.

JUDGE MODE // EVALUATION
1. The Core Problem Solved

During crises, 911 dispatch lines become overwhelmed with conflicting verbal reports. First responders lack immediate visual and geospatial context. DisasterLens turns raw citizen photos into a verifiable, structured triage grid with sub-second AI classification.

2. Computer Vision Pipeline

Multi-head classifier trained on disaster domain datasets for 3 key threats:

  • Fire: Thermal smoke boundaries, flame presence, containment.
  • Structural Damage: Pancake collapse, load-bearing failure, shear stress.
  • Flood: Waterline markers, impassable roadway depth.
  • Severity Scorer: Calibrated Low / Medium / High tri-level matrix.
3. Anti-Spoofing & Zero-Trust Geotagging

Combines browser GPS API, EXIF camera metadata validation, and device magnetometer orientation to eliminate fraudulent out-of-region spam submissions.

4. Premium Visual Foundation (No AI Slop)

Designed intentionally with EOC (Emergency Operations Center) rigor: high-contrast dark palette, hairline borders, telemetry readouts, restrained accent hierarchy (red reserved strictly for high severity), and accessible sans-serif typography.
